# Root da Galera

An app made for the group, so nobody has to keep track in their head of who’s winning at Root anymore.

## What is this?

Root is an asymmetric board game where each player controls a faction with completely different abilities and objectives. It’s tense, chaotic, and inevitably turns into an argument about who’s actually the best player at the table.

This app was built to settle that. The group logs every match, and the system handles everything else: rankings, statistics, and match history — no spreadsheets, no paper notes.

## What it’s for

* Log Root matches between friends, including who played, who won, and which faction they used
* Track the group’s overall victory rankings over time
* View each player’s win rate, both overall and per faction
* Find out who performs best with each faction
* Explore visual match statistics through charts and graphs
* Organize matches into seasons, creating competition cycles with a clear beginning, middle, and end
* Browse the complete history of all matches played
* View an activity log showing everything that was added or deleted

## Features

* **Leaderboard** with rankings by total wins, overall win rate, and total matches played
* **Season and faction filters** — rankings and statistics automatically update based on the active filter
* **Faction-specific win rates** — filter by a specific faction to see each player’s performance using only that faction
* **Visual statistics** with four charts: wins per player (donut), wins per faction (bar), player win rates (bar), and cumulative wins over time (line)
* **Match registration** with support for 4 to 6 participants, player faction selection, and winning faction tracking
* **Faction leaders** — a section showing who has the most victories with each faction in the game
* **Match history** with full records, deletion of incorrect entries, and Excel export support
* **Configurable seasons** to organize competition cycles for the group
* **Terminal-style activity log** showing every created or deleted match, including timestamps and who performed the action
* **Guest access** — anyone can view the leaderboard, history, statistics, and logs without needing a PIN
* **PIN-based login system** to identify who registered each match and unlock write actions
